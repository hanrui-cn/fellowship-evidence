Subject: Responsibility statement — Fellowship application of Mr Rui Han (ref 995181927)

Dear Assessor Team,

My name is Shenyi Song. I worked at ByteDance (TikTok) from 2017 to 2026 and left a few months ago. As Senior Director of VideoArch I led several hundred engineers in charge of the company's global real-time communication and live-streaming technology, and I later served as General Manager of SealSuite, ByteDance's enterprise AI security platform.

I have known Rui Han since March 2020, when I chaired his hiring interview and decided to hire him. We then worked side by side for six years. He led HyperEdge, the edge network that carried the live-streaming traffic for the services my teams ran. I saw it support our biggest events, and I saw it bring our delivery costs down.

Rui Han was an appointed R&D manager. On the R&D management side, he was one of the two managers over about eighty senior engineers at HyperEdge, handling performance reviews, promotion cases and team objectives. On the technical side, he managed more than a million highly distributed and highly heterogeneous edge devices serving the billions of Douyin and TikTok users. On the supplier control side, he managed dozens of third-party suppliers, with admission to the live-traffic pool resting on his personal sign-off.

The clearest impact I saw was the 2022 World Cup final. Rui Han was responsible for the hyper-edge network behind our live streaming, and that night, at the peak, the overall hyper-edge decisions were his. The broadcast stayed stable through the biggest live-streaming peak our platforms had ever carried.

His influence also goes beyond his own programme. Through the university collaborations he set up, he supports other projects, including the end-to-end quality improvement of the RTC project my team owns.

Should you need anything further, you can reach me at the address below.

Yours sincerely,
Shenyi Song
