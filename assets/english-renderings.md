## 14. Pencil News founder interview, 28 Oct 2016 (`entrepreneurship-public-evidence-snapshots-2026-11-21.pdf`, pp.2–4)

**Hong Kong origin (a):**
> 以2013年为界，之前，韩锐在香港开了家教育视频软件公司（LearningTech Ltd.）……他决定直接把香港公司的模式搬到北京……并于2014年年底创立了实时猫。

→ *“Before the 2013 divide, Han Rui ran an education-video software company in Hong Kong (LearningTech Ltd.) … he decided to bring the Hong Kong company's model straight to Beijing … and founded RealTimeCat at the end of 2014.”*

**Why he built the SDK (b):**
> 国外的SDK到了国内便出现严重的水土不服，受移动网络不稳定性、高丢包率以及终端设备差异化等影响，实时视频总出现闪断、掉线等状况。……韩锐决定开发自己的实时视频通信SDK和智能网络技术。……任意网站、App、硬件等在嵌入我们的SDK后，均可连入独有的智能网络，实现高清音视频通话。

→ *“Foreign SDKs badly failed to acclimatise in China: unstable mobile networks, high packet loss and device fragmentation caused constant drops and disconnections … Han Rui decided to develop his own real-time video SDK and intelligent-network technology … Any website, app or device that embeds our SDK connects into a dedicated intelligent network and gains HD audio/video calling.”*

**Private cloud first (c):**
> [小标题] 私有云先行 公有云随后 …… 起初，实时猫只做私有云……但随着技术积累达到一定程度，韩锐觉得公有云才适合做大互联网业务。于是，今年9月，他推出公有云产品。

→ *Section heading: “Private cloud first, public cloud after.” “At first RealTimeCat offered only private clouds — dedicated private video-communication capacity for individual enterprises … as the technology matured, Han Rui concluded the public cloud was the right vehicle for a large internet business, and launched the public-cloud product this September [2016].”*

**Scale and pricing (d):**
> 自公有云试运行2个月后，其月用时长约为8000小时，注册开发机构约有600家。而实时猫则按照视频通讯时长收费，约为1元／小时。

→ *“Two months into the public-cloud trial, monthly usage ran to about 8,000 hours and **about 600 developer organisations had registered**; RealTimeCat charges by communication duration, about RMB 1 per hour.”* [Figure is as of the interview date, 28 October 2016 — not a year-end count.]

**Remote medical consultations (e):**
> 大部分病人居住在偏远的农村……由于医院接入了实时猫的视频通信服务……医生可通过视频通信远程问诊、调整参数等。

→ *“Most patients live in remote rural areas … because the hospital connected to RealTimeCat's video-communication service … doctors can conduct remote consultations and adjust device parameters over the video link.”* (The passage concerns Parkinson's-device patients.)
