To Whom It May Concern,

Re: Letter of Support – Ray Han

I am Dr. Dehui Wei, a Researcher at Microsoft Research Asia. It is my pleasure to write in support of Mr. Ray Han, who served as my manager and mentor at ByteDance from 2022 to 2026.

I joined Ray Han's team at ByteDance in 2022 as a research intern while completing my PhD at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). He served as my direct manager for approximately two years and mentored me through the BUPT–ByteDance joint research project. He later recommended me for a postdoctoral position at the National University of Singapore (NUS). After I moved there, we continued working together on the NUS–ByteDance joint project, where Ray served as my industry collaborator; my postdoctoral supervision remained with my NUS faculty advisor. Across these joint projects, I transitioned from campus research to hands-on work on a real, large-scale hyper-edge multimedia network system.

During my time at ByteDance, we met one to three times a week, and he set my goals in our weekly meetings. He worked closely with me on every hyper-edge paper, from the earliest idea to the final draft. He helped me decide what each paper was really about and which systems methods to use. Most of our time went into the writing, and he made sure I explained clearly what we had learned and why it mattered. These papers include "Pscheduler" at IEEE INFOCOM 2024, "Multipath Smart Preloading" in IEEE Network 2024, and "HyperEdge," a first-authored paper of mine that received the Best Paper Award at USENIX NSDI 2026. Early on, he trusted me with real responsibility, letting me lead part of the technical work and mentor master's students on my own while staying available to coach me. He also cared about my career, not just the research, and talked through every major decision with me.

His mentorship directly changed the trajectory of my career. I completed my PhD at BUPT, served as a postdoctoral researcher at NUS, and am now a Researcher at Microsoft Research. The papers I published along the way grew out of the problems he gave me and the way he trained me to work through them. He also brought me into a real-time communication networks project and guided my work on it. Today, I mentor younger researchers myself in the same practical, hands-on way I learned from him.

I also observed him mentoring many others. During my time there, approximately ten graduates from the BUPT–ByteDance joint programme, along with students from other university collaborations, worked under his mentorship. He helped them become stronger researchers and engineers, and their projects produced work of practical impact.

I give Ray Han my strongest, unreserved support in any application or endeavor he may pursue.

For any further information please feel free to contact me by email at dehuiwei@microsoft.com
