## B. Supporter statement (Reasons for selection)

I am a professor at the Institute of Computing Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, where I lead the Network Architecture and System Group. I have known Mr. Rui Han for about four years, since a senior technical leader at ByteDance introduced us to explore a research collaboration. I am writing to support his application based on his work in invention and innovation, as well as mentoring and coaching.

When we began collaborating, Mr. Han was leading the development of what he termed HyperEdge networking. He approached my group to help optimize the cost of ByteDance's edge live-streaming system. I was initially struck by the fact that he had already taken this system from a conceptual architecture to a massive production reality. This request quickly evolved into a formal university-industry collaboration between his engineering team and my research group.

To advance this work, I sent my PhD student, Tian Yu, to his team for a one-year internship. Mr. Han personally mentored her inside ByteDance. He provided the engineering resources she needed, paired her with master's students from other universities, and met with her regularly to tackle the hard problems of transport protocols, flow control, and connection management in a live system. By the end of that year, she was independently solving production-grade network challenges. This joint effort produced the RLive paper, published at EuroSys 2026, and gave my own group a new edge CDN research capability.

Mr. Han's influence in systems and networking comes directly from his success in making home edge networking viable at commercial scale. He has established deep collaborations with multiple universities, effectively shaping how the industry approaches distributed delivery. Among the industry researchers I collaborate with, he stands out for his ability to organize rigorous research while innovating under intense production pressure. I have reviewed his statements on invention and mentoring, which accurately reflect the reality I observed. I fully support his election to BCS Fellow.
