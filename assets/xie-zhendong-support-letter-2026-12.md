To Whom It May Concern:

**Re: Letter of Support for Mr. Ray Han**

I am writing in support of Mr. Ray Han. I am currently a Senior Engineer in China Commerce and Advertising at ByteDance, where I have worked for seven years. From 2022 to 2026, I worked in the HyperEdge Networking group and reported directly to him for four and a half years. My work focused on network probing and measurement, and I worked directly with him on the systems and research detailed in this letter.

As a Principal Engineer at ByteDance, Mr. Han directs the overall system architecture, edge-node development, and resource management for HyperEdge Networking. HyperEdge operates ByteDance's low-cost content delivery network, powering video-on-demand and live-streaming services for Douyin and TikTok across domestic and international markets.

**He innovates at the infrastructure level to unlock new capabilities.** For five years, HyperEdge operated strictly as a downstream content delivery network. As ByteDance needed to monitor access quality across its global media network, Mr. Han recognized that the massive fleet of managed edge nodes close to household networks provided observation points that conventional data-center monitoring could not offer. Rather than building a separate monitoring fleet, he led our team through the architectural overhaul required to turn passive delivery nodes into active measurement vantage points. Under his guidance, we advanced our capabilities from basic ping probes to complex network-protocol, streaming-media, and bottleneck tests. This established three core capabilities for ByteDance: stability monitoring, real-time measurement, and simulated-user measurement. What began as an infrastructure refactoring grew into a dedicated engineering division that today forms the operational foundation for ByteDance's AI web-fetching systems.

**He translates production-scale challenges into foundational research breakthroughs.** As our edge systems scaled, our team encountered production bottlenecks in scheduling and measurement that could not be resolved through routine debugging or engineering experience alone. Mr. Han recognized that these problems demanded a fundamental research approach and initiated a joint project with Professor Yiming Qiu's group at the University of Hong Kong. He defined the technical problems, walked researchers through our production constraints, and required theoretical models to be validated against deployed conditions. This work eliminated our scheduling bottlenecks and produced two major papers: *Horizon* at ACM SIGCOMM 2026, which I coauthored with him, and *Skyline* at USENIX NSDI 2026.

He also partnered with the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT) to deploy measurement probes across residential routers and set-top boxes. Capturing telemetry directly from home devices resolved a critical blind spot between server-side metrics and last-mile user experience.

**He develops engineers into system owners and technical leaders.** Mr. Han divides the group's work into clear domains so each engineer has defined ownership and room to grow. Rather than centralizing technical authority, he encourages team members to take full responsibility for core subsystems and solve problems at the system level.

In early 2023, when I was preparing for promotion from Engineer to Senior Engineer, I was not yet competitive against the standard for that level. Rather than offering vague encouragement, Mr. Han worked out a promotion roadmap with me, set clear milestones, and reviewed my progress regularly. His technical guidance helped me close the gaps in my work and take on broader architectural scope. Once I met those milestones, he helped build support for my case across company promotion committees, and my promotion went through. Other engineers across the group experienced the same mentorship and advanced under his guidance.

Over the four and a half years I reported to him, Mr. Han consistently proved his ability to solve hard engineering problems at massive scale and develop the talent around him to sustain them. Based on this direct experience, I support his application without reservation.

Please contact me if I can be of further assistance.
