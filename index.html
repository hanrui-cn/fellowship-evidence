Invention and innovation

Submitted statement

Situation

By 2020, short video and live streaming served billions of users, mostly on mobile. Video delivery had shifted from delay-tolerant downloads to streaming. Central CDN nodes could no longer carry this traffic economically. The industry turned to household routers and set-top boxes, whose hardware, location and bandwidth varied widely. Existing P2P-CDN systems were built for downloads and could not stream reliably.

Task

I set out to make these heterogeneous, unstable devices deliver CDN-grade streaming at production scale. I originated HyperEdge at ByteDance, drawing on earlier real-time P2P delivery work at LearningTech and distributed-storage work at MASS.

Action

I created a multidimensional capability-homogenization method that represents diverse devices as a uniform pool of usable capacity. It tracks each device's hardware, location and service profile as devices join, change or fail, giving the scheduler a consistent resource model.

I then designed a receiver-driven multi-source transport protocol. The receiving client requests byte ranges from several sources, controls each path separately and moves unfinished requests when a source stalls.

Using these methods, I developed one delivery framework for on-demand, live and real-time media. For on-demand video, I designed CDN-edge coordination for fast start-up and fallback to the CDN when edge resources cannot sustain playback. For live streaming, I built a redundancy-free architecture combining distributed frame sequencing with playback-deadline loss recovery.

I extended these methods to media-network measurement and distributed inference.

Result

R1 Scale, cost and performance. HyperEdge serves over two billion daily active users on TikTok and Douyin, delivering up to 60 Tbps from over 500,000 devices. During the 2022 World Cup, the network absorbed 80 Tbps of burst traffic without straining the ISP backbone. It has saved over three billion US dollars against commercial CDN pricing. In A/B tests, it matched commercial CDN performance. The live system tripled capacity and cut rebuffering by 15%.

R2 Standards leadership. At the China Communications Standards Association (CCSA), China's national communications standards body, I am a drafter of two draft standards covering transmission feedback and cross-layer bitrate adaptation. I work on these with Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications and Xi'an Jiaotong University. I lead two further drafts awaiting ministry review, with China Unicom, one of China's three national carriers, as a joint drafter.

R3 Research and patents. The work yielded 18 China and U.S. patents, four as first or sole inventor. It helped secure RMB 3 million under China's National Key R&D Program (2022–2025), with HyperEdge serving as the validation platform. The HyperEdge paper won the USENIX NSDI 2026 Best Paper Award, with five further papers from my team at SIGCOMM 2026, EuroSys 2026, INFOCOM 2024, IEEE Network and IWQoS 2025.

Mentoring and coaching

Submitted statement

Situation

HyperEdge required expertise across device systems, network protocols and resource management. There was no established talent pool for this new architecture.

Task

I set out to recruit and develop new graduates, experienced hires and partner-university researchers until they could independently lead a subsystem or research project.

Action

I built the ByteDance team by recruiting across adjacent fields, conducting more than a hundred interviews in my first year. I trained media researchers in network transport and transport engineers in distributed delivery. I gave each engineer a distinct part of HyperEdge to own. Over several years, I worked alongside them, guiding their technical work and reviewing their progress until they could set technical direction independently. I also sponsored their promotions.

I established long-running partnerships with universities including Fudan, BUPT, HKU and NUS. Through these partnerships, I matched graduate researchers to production problems and guided them in framing research questions, choosing methods and writing papers.

Result

R1 Engineering leadership. Three engineers I mentored over several years reached Staff Engineer, ByteDance's senior technical-lead grade. Each now leads a sub-team responsible for one of the edge-media, edge-infrastructure and edge-protocol subsystems. More than ten others advanced into senior roles across the platform.

R2 Research career progression. Dr Wei Dehui spent two years under my supervision during her BUPT doctorate. Her work led directly to the HyperEdge paper, on which she is first author. On my recommendation, she took a postdoctoral fellowship at NUS and subsequently became a researcher at Microsoft Research Asia.

R3 Research output. I co-supervised more than ten graduate researchers through ByteDance's university partnerships. Their work on HyperEdge and related network topics appeared at SIGCOMM, NSDI, INFOCOM, IEEE Network and EuroSys.

Entrepreneurship

Submitted statement

Situation

In the early 2010s, online teaching and embedded video communication were growing, but proprietary systems were costly and WebRTC was difficult to deploy reliably. Schools and businesses faced substantial engineering work before they could use browser-based audio and video in their own services.

Task

I set out to turn WebRTC into a communication service that schools, developers and enterprises could adopt without building their own infrastructure.

Action

I co-founded LearningTech in Hong Kong in 2013 and led it as CEO until June 2018.

I led development of one of China's first public WebRTC SDKs and the software-defined network that powered our real-time audio and video services. I designed the network to carry and manage calls from customer websites and applications connected through the SDK, including cross-border communication.

I commercialised the platform through enterprise private-cloud deployments and a public cloud service priced by call duration. I used seed and angel funding to expand the network, grow the engineering team and deliver customer projects.

After over five years of operation, I exited in 2018 through the acquisition of our technology, product and engineering team by Baijiayun (now NASDAQ: RTC).

Result

R1 National recognition. LearningTech earned China's National High-Tech Enterprise accreditation in October 2017, following expert assessment of our intellectual property, R&D investment and revenue from high-tech products and services.

R2 Developer adoption at scale. Our SDK ranked first in its field for annual downloads, establishing LearningTech as an early leader in China's WebRTC developer market. By the end of 2016, our public cloud served more than 600 registered developer organizations. Our MIT-licensed JavaScript SDK recorded over 26,000 npm downloads from its 2015 release through November 2026.

R3 Commercial success. The business raised RMB 8.5 million in seed and angel funding. Our paying enterprise customers included China General Technology Group and Motorola Solutions. Our online teaching service supported over 1.76 million user sessions across more than 184,000 virtual classrooms.

LearningTech's products were marketed as RealTimeCat and Linctime Class.
